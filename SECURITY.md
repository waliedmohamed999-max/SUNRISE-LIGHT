# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to **info@sunriselight.sa** with the subject
"Security report". Do not open a public issue and do not include customer data in your report.

We acknowledge reports within 3 working days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.
Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before any public disclosure.

## Scope

The Sunrise Light web platform (website, design studio, customer accounts, factory dashboard and APIs).
Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering, physical attacks and third-party services.
Only test against your own accounts; never access, modify or delete other people's data.
